# Corpus catalog gaps — what the community names that the catalog can't yet match

*Backlog — 2026-09-19, from the A10 seed run (campaign `a10-corpus-seed-20260919`, 125 boosts
applied). Source: the LLM mention inventory over 2,449 community emails
([`research/report-corpus-classification.md`](../research/report-corpus-classification.md) § Where
corpus tooling lives). Counts are messages that mention the place; everything here is a derived
insight (L5a), no post text.*

Three lists, three owners. The rivers are deliberately absent — D4 deferred them, and the Phase 01 roadmap entry now carries
the measured demand: Connecticut River 61 messages, Dead Creek 21, the first numbers behind
river reaches.

## 1. Bays and reaches to draw — 76, of which 16 are destinations

The parent matched; the bay is not drawn. **None has an OSM polygon** (A07a already imported every
one that did); 39 exist as a point, the rest are spelling variants, landmarks or prose locatives.
Automatic outlining from the point was tried (disc, then narrowest-chord) and found a real mouth
on 1 of 39 — retired. The corpus splits the 39 by *skated* messages: **16 destinations** (skated in
≥ 2 messages) are the queue for [`features/subarea-chord-editor.md`](../features/subarea-chord-editor.md);
**23 reference points** become labels through [`features/named-landmarks.md`](../features/named-landmarks.md).
